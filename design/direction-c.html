Leek & feta tart

Pâte brisée, soft leeks and a savoury custard. A Sunday tart that keeps.

season
Autumn, into winter
cuisine
frFrench
course
Main
diet
Vegetarian
makes
6 slices · 1 h 40
pastry
flour240 g
cold butter160 g
ice water80 g
filling
leeks3
eggs3
crème fraîche300 g
feta120 g

  1. Rub 240 g flour and ½ tsp salt with 160 g cold butter, cubed, until the pieces are the size of peas. Stir in 80 g ice water with a fork until it just holds.

  2. Press into a flat disc, wrap, and chill.

  3. Roll to 3 mm, line a 23 cm tin and blind-bake at 190 °C with paper and beans.

  4. Meanwhile, slice 3 leeks into thin coins and sweat them in 30 g butter with a pinch of salt until soft and sweet, not browned.

  5. Whisk 3 eggs with 300 g crème fraîche, a grating of nutmeg and black pepper.

  6. Scatter the leeks and 120 g feta over the shell, pour in the custard and bake at 170 °C until just set, with a slight wobble.

  7. Let it rest before slicing. Good warm, better at room temperature.

Notes, photos & tags

Oct ’25 — used half feta, half goat’s cheese. Blind-bake a little longer in the old oven.

Pastry

  • 240 g plain flour
  • 160 g cold butter, cubed
  • 80 g ice water
  • ½ tsp fine salt

Filling

  • 3 leeks
  • 30 g butter
  • 3 eggs
  • 300 g crème fraîche
  • 120 g feta
  • nutmeg, pepper

Method

Rub the flour and salt with the butter until pea-sized; stir in the water until it just holds. Press into a disc, wrap and chill for 30 minutes.

Roll to 3 mm, line a 23 cm tin and blind-bake at 190 °C for 20 minutes. Meanwhile sweat the sliced leeks in butter with a pinch of salt, about 12 minutes.

Whisk the eggs, crème fraîche, nutmeg and pepper. Fill the shell with leeks and feta, pour over the custard and bake at 170 °C for 30–35 minutes. Rest 10 minutes.
