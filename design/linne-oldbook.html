Old book

Aged paper, sepia ink and bookbinding cloth: the feel of the family cookbook that lives by the stove, its spine cracked open at the tart page.

  • Paper is a warm, slightly yellowed cream. A faint laid-paper grain lives only in the page margins; every card you read from is flat paper.
  • Oxblood cloth is the one action colour: buttons, the selected view and the ribbon bookmark on the step you are on.
  • Bottle-green cloth is for “good” things: what you already have, approvals, finished timers, and the focus ring.

Leek & feta tart

Crisp pastry, soft leeks and a savoury custard. A Sunday tart that keeps.

Serves 61 h 40 minFrom Savta Rina

Amounts in grams

You’ll need

Pastry

  • flour 240 g
  • cold butter 160 g
  • ice water 80 g
  • salt ½ tsp

Filling

  • leeks 3
  • butter 30 g
  • eggs 3
  • crème fraîche 300 g
  • feta 120 g
  • nutmeg, pepper a little
  1. 1Step 1 of 7

    Rub 240 g flour and ½ tsp salt with 160 g cold butter, cubed, until the pieces are the size of peas. Stir in 80 g ice water with a fork until it just holds.

  2. 2Step 2 of 7

    Press into a flat disc, wrap, and chill.

  3. 3Step 3 of 7

    Roll to 3 mm, line a 23 cm tin and blind-bake at 190 °C with paper and beans.

  4. 4Step 4 of 7You are here

    Meanwhile, slice 3 leeks into thin coins and cook them gently in 30 g butter with a pinch of salt until soft and sweet, not browned.


  5. 5Step 5 of 7You are here

    Whisk 3 eggs with 300 g crème fraîche, a grating of nutmeg and black pepper.

  6. 6Step 6 of 7You are here

    Scatter the leeks and 120 g feta over the shell, pour in the custard and bake at 170 °C until just set, with a slight wobble.


  7. 7Step 7 of 7You are here

    Let it rest before slicing. Good warm, better at room temperature.


Notes, photos & tags

Oct ’25: half feta, half goat’s cheese. Blind-bake 5 minutes longer in the old oven.

Ingredients

  • 240 g plain flour
  • 160 g cold butter
  • 80 g ice water
  • 3 leeks
  • 3 eggs
  • 300 g crème fraîche
  • 120 g feta

Method

Rub the flour and salt with the butter until pea-sized; stir in the water. Chill 30 minutes. Roll, line a 23 cm tin and blind-bake at 190 °C for 20 minutes.

Soften the leeks in butter. Whisk eggs, crème fraîche and nutmeg; fill the shell with leeks and feta, pour over and bake at 170 °C for 30–35 minutes.
